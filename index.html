Questions about OBO

What is ONE BY ONE?

ONE BY ONE (OBO) is free desktop software by Pollyne Studios for creating mixed-media animation. It connects digital frames with hands-on drawing, painting and collage on paper.

How do I animate on paper with OBO?

Import a video or image sequence, arrange the frames and check the motion in the preview. OBO lays out your frames on printable sheets that you export as a PDF. Draw, paint or collage on the printed frames, then scan the sheets and bring them back into OBO. Adjust the alignment, review the frames and export your finished animation. Explore the step-by-step tutorials.

What can I use on the printed frames?

Draw, paint, collage or combine materials directly on the printed frames. Each frame becomes part of the final animation, so you can experiment with textures and handmade details.

How do I bring my paper animation back into OBO?

Scan your edited sheets with all printed markers and codes visible. Import the scans into OBO, adjust their alignment and review the sequence before exporting. Keep the sheet references intact when printing and working on the paper.

Is OBO free, and do I need an account?

OBO is free to download and use, with no subscription or account required. Project files stay on your device. You can support development with a voluntary contribution.

Does OBO work on Mac and Windows?

OBO runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later, and on 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11 computers. Download OBO for your computer.
