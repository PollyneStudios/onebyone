How OBO works

01 / Import

Start with a video or image sequence. Arrange the frames and check the motion in the preview.

02 / Print

OBO arranges your frames on printable sheets. Export the PDF and print it.

03 / Create

You can draw, paint, collage, and do much more. Work on each frame by hand and make the sequence your own.

04 / Reanimate

Scan your edited sheets. Adjust the alignment, check your frames and export the animation.
