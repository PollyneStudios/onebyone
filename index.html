How OBO works

01 / Import

Start with a video or image sequence. Arrange the frames and check the motion in the preview.

02 / Print

OBO arranges your frames on printable sheets. Export the PDF and print it.

03 / Create

You can draw, paint, collage, and do much more. Work on each frame by hand and make the sequence your own.

04 / Reanimate

Scan your edited sheets. Adjust the alignment, check your frames and export the animation.

Tutorial

01 / New Project

Create a new project and give your animation a home.

02 / Import

Start with a video or image sequence.

03 / Frames

Arrange your frames and check the motion in the preview.

04 / Sheets

Set up your paper and frame layout. Check the preview before exporting the PDF. Preserve all references when printing and avoid cutting out markers and codes.

05 / Create

Work on each frame by hand and make the sequence your own.

06 / Scans

Scan your edited sheets and bring them back into OBO.

07 / Review

Adjust the alignment and check your frames.

08 / Export

Export your finished animation.
