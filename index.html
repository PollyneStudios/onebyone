Questions about OBO

What is ONE BY ONE?

ONE BY ONE (OBO) is free desktop software by Pollyne Studios for creating mixed-media animation. It connects digital frames with hands-on drawing, painting and collage on paper.

How do I animate on paper with OBO?

Import a video or image sequence, arrange the frames and export printable PDF sheets. Decorate the frames by hand, scan your sheets, then use OBO to align, review and export the animation. Explore the step-by-step tutorials.

Is OBO free, and do I need an account?

OBO is free to download and use, with no subscription or account required. Project files stay on your device. You can support development with a voluntary contribution.

Does OBO work on Mac and Windows?

OBO runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later, and on 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11 computers. Download OBO for your computer.
