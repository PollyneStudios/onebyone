Privacy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice covers obo-onebyone.com and enquiries or support connected with this website. It does not describe every operation performed by the OBO desktop application.

Who runs this site

Pietro Cazzago runs this website as a private individual under the Pollyne Studios project name. For privacy requests and website enquiries, email pollyne.studios@gmail.com.

When you visit

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends GitHub technical information needed to deliver the pages, including your IP address. GitHub also keeps security logs. The homepage contacts GitHub’s API to check the latest available downloads; downloading an installer connects you to GitHub as well.

We use these services to make the website and downloads available and protect them from misuse, based on our legitimate interest in running a reliable website. GitHub may process technical data outside the European Economic Area. It explains its processing, retention and international transfer safeguards in its privacy statement: GitHub privacy statement

Cookies and tracking

The site does not set analytics or advertising cookies, build visitor profiles, or use automated decision-making. Fonts, tutorial footage and preview images are served with the site. Instagram and Ko-fi are links, rather than embedded widgets: their pages load when you choose to open them.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this information to answer your enquiry, based on our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact the project. Providing it is optional, but we need a way to reply. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any related follow-up or legal obligation. It is not added to a mailing list.

If you support OBO

The Support OBO link opens Ko-fi. Payments are handled there through its payment provider, rather than on this website. As the recipient, we may receive supporter information such as a name, email address, message, amount and payment status. We use it to manage your support, answer related questions and meet applicable accounting obligations. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in administering voluntary support and, where applicable, compliance with legal obligations.

Support records are retained while needed to manage the payment or a dispute, and for any retention period required by applicable accounting law. Ko-fi and the payment provider have their own privacy notices: Ko-fi privacy notice and PayPal privacy notice

Your choices and rights

You can contact the operator above to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Portability applies where its legal conditions are met. We may need enough information to verify that the request is yours.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali: Garante privacy authority

Changes

If the site starts using new services or collecting different information, this page will be updated. The date above identifies the current notice.
